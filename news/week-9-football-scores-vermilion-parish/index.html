Week 9 Scoreboard: Kaplan Holds Off Abbeville, Erath and North Vermilion Roll

Final scores for every Vermilion Parish high school football team from Thursday and Friday night.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Oct. 31, 2026, 8:45 a.m.

Kaplan beat old rival Abbeville 43-32 on Thursday night, and Erath and North Vermilion both won big on Friday, according to final scores posted by KATC and Acadiana News First. Here is how every Vermilion Parish team fared in Week 9.

Thursday

  • Kaplan 43, Abbeville 32. The Advocate reported the win kept the Russo Trophy in Kaplan.
  • Elton 53, Gueydan 20.

Friday

  • Erath 42, St. Martinville 14.
  • North Vermilion 49, LaGrange 14.
  • Lafayette Christian Academy 49, Delcambre 0.
  • Ascension Episcopal over Vermilion Catholic. The Advocate reported Ascension Episcopal won to keep its district title hopes alive; a final score was not listed on the KATC scoreboard.
