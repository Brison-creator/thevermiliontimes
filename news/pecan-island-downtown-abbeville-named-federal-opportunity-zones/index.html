Pecan Island and Downtown Abbeville Named Federal Opportunity Zones, Opening Tax Breaks for Investors

Two Vermilion Parish areas made the state’s new list. One covers the whole coast, including Pecan Island, where SpaceX plans to build. The breaks start Jan. 1.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 24, 2026, 6:50 a.m.

Two parts of Vermilion Parish are on Louisiana’s new list of federal Opportunity Zones, places where investors can get tax breaks for putting money into businesses and property.

One of them takes in the entire coast of the parish, including Pecan Island, where SpaceX plans to start building in 2027. The other is the middle of Abbeville.

The state announced 156 zones on Nov. 13 but did not list them by town. The Vermilion Times pulled the list from the state’s own map and checked the boundaries.

The two zones here

  • The coast (Census Tract 9511). It stretches across the south end of the parish along the Gulf. Pecan Island, Forked Island, Esther, Mouton Cove, Intracoastal City and Freshwater City are all inside it.
  • Central Abbeville (Census Tract 9507). A small area, about two miles across, that includes the courthouse square downtown.

Kaplan, Gueydan, Erath, Delcambre and Maurice are not in a zone.

Both Vermilion zones are classed as rural, which matters: the state says the renewed program gives extra incentives for investments in rural zones.

What an Opportunity Zone is

It is a federal tax break for investors. Someone who has made a profit selling stock, land or a business can put that money into a project inside a zone, through what the law calls a Qualified Opportunity Fund, and pay less federal tax on it.

The state lists these benefits starting Jan. 1, 2027:

  • Tax on the original profit is put off.
  • The taxable amount is cut by 10%.
  • Growth in the new investment can be tax-free.
  • Rural zones get a bigger incentive and easier rules for fixing up property.

The Treasury Department says the new zones last 10 years.

What it is not

It is not a grant, and it does not pay residents or the parish anything directly. It does not change local property taxes. The idea is to make it cheaper for private money to build things in these places: housing, shops, hotels, industrial sites.

Whether that happens is up to investors. The designation only makes the area eligible.

The bigger picture

  • Statewide: 156 zones, 82 of them rural. Louisiana nominated the most it was allowed to, and all were approved, the state said.
  • Acadiana: 22 zones. Lafayette Parish has nine, St. Landry four, and Vermilion, Iberia, Acadia and St. Martin two each. Evangeline has one.
  • Who picked them: The state says it worked through regional economic development groups, with input from local officials, business leaders and developers.

The coast zone lands as SpaceX prepares a launch site the company says will bring more than 3,000 jobs. [SpaceX is taking resumes now, and local businesses can sign up as suppliers.](/news/spacex-taking-resumes-starbase-louisiana-email-supplier-signup/)

How we checked: We downloaded the 156 tracts from the state’s Opportunity Zone map, found the two in Vermilion Parish and tested the location of each town against the tract boundaries.
