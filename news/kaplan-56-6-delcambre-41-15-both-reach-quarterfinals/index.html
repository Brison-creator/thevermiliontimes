Kaplan Rolls 56-6 and Delcambre Wins Again: Both Parish Teams Reach the Quarterfinals

Kaplan routed Vidalia at home. Delcambre, the No. 25 seed, beat No. 9 Grand Lake 41-15 and now draws the top seed in its bracket.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 21, 2026, 6:05 a.m.

Vermilion Parish went 2-for-2 on Friday night, and neither game was close.

Kaplan 56, Vidalia 6

No. 4 Kaplan opened its playoff run by burying No. 13 Vidalia 56-6 at home in the regional round of Division III Non-Select, according to the Louisiana High School Athletic Association’s bracket. Kaplan had a first-round bye, so this was its first game of the postseason.

Next: Kaplan plays No. 12 Green Oaks in the quarterfinals. Green Oaks got there by edging No. 5 Marksville 30-26.

Delcambre 41, Grand Lake 15

The run continues for No. 25 Delcambre. One week after beating No. 8 South Plaquemines by a single point, Delcambre went back on the road and handled No. 9 Grand Lake 41-15 in Division IV Non-Select.

That’s two straight road wins over top-10 seeds for the No. 25 seed.

Next: the top seed. Delcambre plays No. 1 East Feliciana, which beat No. 16 West St. Mary 38-12 on Friday after a first-round bye.

When they play

The quarterfinals are next weekend. The LHSAA bracket had not posted dates, times or sites for either parish team’s game as of Saturday morning. We’ll post them when it does.
