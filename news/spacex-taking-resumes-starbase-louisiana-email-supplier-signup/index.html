SpaceX Is Taking Resumes for Starbase Louisiana Now. Here’s the Email, and How Local Businesses Sign Up for the Work

The company says it will hire “heavily from nearby communities.” Its Louisiana page has a resume button, and there are two free sign-ups for contractors and suppliers.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 23, 2026, 7:10 a.m.

You do not have to wait for a job posting to get your name in front of SpaceX.

The company’s own page for Starbase, Louisiana, has a “Submit a Resume” button. It opens an email to a hiring address set up for the Louisiana site.

If you want a job

Email your resume to starbaselajobs@spacex.com.

That is the address behind the button on SpaceX’s Starbase, LA page. The button fills in the subject line “Joining the Starbase, LA team” and a short note saying you are sending a resume and an introduction.

On the same page, SpaceX says it is committed to “creating more than 3,000 new jobs” at the site, “hiring heavily from nearby communities” and using Louisiana workers’ experience building complex projects in this environment.

What the page does not say: which jobs the resumes are for, what they pay, or when hiring will start. SpaceX lists construction as starting in 2027 and the first Starship launch in 2029.

Jobs with full descriptions are posted on a separate careers board. There were 12 for Pecan Island as of this morning, the same 12 as Saturday. [Here is every one and how to apply.](/news/every-spacex-job-open-pecan-island-12-positions-how-to-apply/)

If you own a business

There are two places to sign up. Both are free.

1. SpaceX’s supplier page. Go to spacex.com/supplier and choose “Become a Supplier.” SpaceX says it builds most of its own hardware but often uses “specialized, small, nimble, or local suppliers.”

2. The state’s Source Louisiana directory. Louisiana Economic Development says SpaceX has joined Source Louisiana, which it describes as a pathway for Louisiana suppliers, contractors and service providers to compete for work on the project. Register at louisiana.supplierone.co. The state lists three steps:

  • Create a free business profile.
  • List what your company can do, plus its certifications and services.
  • Your profile becomes visible to major projects and their buyers.

The state says more than 4,000 Louisiana suppliers and contractors are in the directory. It also says plainly that signing up is no guarantee of work.

A training program for contractors

The state also runs a six-month program that gets small and mid-sized companies ready to bid on big-project work. The first class, 85 graduates, was honored last week. The state says companies can sign up now to be told when applications for the 2027 class open in January.

The numbers behind it

The state’s page on the project says SpaceX is expected to create 3,000 direct jobs over 10 years at an average salary of $92,600, and estimates another 8,100 jobs for local contractors and vendors.

It costs nothing to send a resume or register a business. Be careful with anyone who asks for money to get you hired.
