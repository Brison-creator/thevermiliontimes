Four Seconds Short: Texas Beats LSU 22-21 on Last-Gasp Touchdown in Death Valley

The Tigers led most of the night behind two interceptions from safety Ty Benefield, but Arch Manning hit Raleek Brown from 4 yards out with four seconds left. LSU is 6-4 and heads to Tennessee on Saturday.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 16, 2026, 6:15 a.m.

LSU had Texas beat for almost the whole night. Then the clock showed four seconds.

On Saturday in Tiger Stadium, Texas quarterback Arch Manning threw a 4-yard touchdown pass to Raleek Brown with four seconds left, and the No. 6 Longhorns escaped Death Valley with a 22-21 win, according to LSU Athletics. It was the Tigers’ home finale.

How LSU built the lead

Texas scored first on a Manning 1-yard run. LSU answered right away: quarterback Sam Leavitt hit receiver Jackson Harris for a 10-yard touchdown to finish a 13-play, 75-yard drive.

Then safety Ty Benefield took over. He picked off Manning and ran it back 33 yards to the Texas 7, and Leavitt and Harris connected for a second score, this one from 9 yards, to put LSU up 14-7. LSU led 14-10 at the half after a missed 38-yard field goal.

Benefield intercepted Manning a second time in the third quarter, and running back Dilin Jones broke loose for a 44-yard touchdown run to make it 21-10.

How it slipped away

Texas kicker Gianni Spetic chipped away with field goals of 41 and 50 yards, the first set up when LSU defensive end Princewill Umanmielen sacked Manning. The lead was down to five, 21-16, early in the fourth quarter. LSU’s offense didn’t score again, and Manning’s late strike to Brown won it.

Benefield’s line: two picks, a sack and eight tackles. Umanmielen also had eight tackles.

What’s next

The loss is LSU’s second straight, after a 31-13 defeat to Alabama the week before. Lane Kiffin’s Tigers are 6-4 overall and 3-4 in the SEC.

LSU finishes the regular season on the road: at Tennessee on Saturday, Nov. 21, at 2:30 p.m. Central, then at Arkansas on Nov. 28.
