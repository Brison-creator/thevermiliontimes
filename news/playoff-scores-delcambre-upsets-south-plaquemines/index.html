Delcambre Stuns No. 8 South Plaquemines 19-18 to Advance; Kaplan Gets Vidalia Next Week

The 25th-seeded Delcambre won by a single point on the road. Erath, North Vermilion and Vermilion Catholic saw their seasons end in the first round, according to the LHSAA’s official brackets.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 13, 2026, 11:20 p.m.

Delcambre is still playing football.

The No. 25 seed went on the road and beat No. 8 South Plaquemines 19-18 in the first round of the Division IV Non-Select playoffs, according to the Louisiana High School Athletic Association’s official bracket. One point was the difference between a long bus ride home and a trip to the regional round.

It was the only first-round win for a Vermilion Parish team.

First-round scores

  • Delcambre 19, South Plaquemines 18 (Division IV Non-Select). Delcambre advances.
  • Northwest 31, Erath 16 (Division II Non-Select). Erath’s season ends.
  • Lutcher 55, North Vermilion 14 (Division II Non-Select). North Vermilion’s season ends.
  • St. John 48, Vermilion Catholic 27 (Division IV Select). Vermilion Catholic’s season ends.

Who’s next

  • Delcambre (No. 25) plays at No. 9 Grand Lake in the regional round.
  • Kaplan (No. 4, Division III Non-Select), which had a first-round bye, will host No. 13 Vidalia, which won its first-round game against Lakeside.

The regional round is set for Nov. 19-21. The LHSAA lists date ranges; schools set the exact kickoff times, and we’ll post them when the brackets do.
