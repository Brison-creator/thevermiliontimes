Who’s Hiring Near Vermilion Parish This Week: Welders, a Dump Truck Driver in Abbeville, Mechanics and More

Ten trade jobs within driving distance, pulled from the state’s official job site. Two list pay. One is in Abbeville.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 23, 2026, 1:05 p.m.

The state runs a free job site, LouisianaNOW.jobs, where employers post openings. We searched it Monday for every town in Vermilion Parish and the towns next door.

One job came up in the parish itself, in Abbeville. Nothing was listed for Kaplan, Erath, Gueydan, Delcambre or Maurice. Broussard, Youngsville and New Iberia had plenty.

Here are the trade jobs, closest first. Each link goes to the listing, where you can apply.

In Vermilion Parish

Dump Truck Operator, Heritage Shell & Limestone, Abbeville Hauls sand, dirt, limestone and other material to construction sites and customers. Needs a Class A or Class B commercial license and a clean driving record. Dump truck experience is preferred but not required. No pay listed. See the listing

Broussard

Welder II, HB Rentals (Superior Energy Services) Shop welder and fitter building and repairing offshore housing units. Must be certified to 6GR or the equivalent and able to read blueprints. No pay listed. See the listing

Carpenter/Joiner, HB Rentals (Superior Energy Services) Builds out and repairs housing units: insulation, paneling, cutting material to size. High school diploma or GED. Two to three years of experience preferred, but the listing says the company is willing to train. No pay listed. See the listing

Turbine Engine Technician Apprentice, Cadorath Takes apart and rebuilds turbine engines and parts. A federal power plant certificate is preferred but not required. Drug test required. No pay listed. See the listing

Maintenance Technician, Superior Energy Services Inspects and repairs mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems and shop equipment. No pay listed. See the listing

Welder, Sub Arc, Global Vessel and Tank (Broussard and Lafayette) Shop welder for a tank and vessel maker. Reads blueprints, sets up parts and welds to procedure. No pay listed. See the listing

Youngsville

Mechanic, Elwood Staffing: $20 to $25 an hour Temp-to-hire, Monday through Friday, 6 a.m. to 4 p.m. Repairs forklifts, cranes and shop equipment. Asks for four years of experience, with forklift repair shown on your resume. Background check and drug screen. See the listing

Welder/Fabricator II, night shift, Leading Edge Manufacturing Custom metal fabrication shop. No pay shown in the listing summary. See the listing

New Iberia

Pipe Fitter/Pipe Welder, Global Vessel and Tank The listing says pay depends on experience and that employees get company stock through an employee ownership plan. See the listing

GSE Mechanic, Avex MRO: $20 to $30 Works on the ground equipment at an aircraft maintenance shop: diesel, gas and electric engines, hydraulics and electrical systems. High school diploma or GED. Background check and drug screen. The listing gives the pay range as $20 to $30 and does not say per what. See the listing

Before you apply

  • These are the employers’ own postings on the state’s site. We did not call the companies, and the listings do not show the date they were posted. A job can fill at any time.
  • Applying is free. Be careful with anyone who asks for money to get you hired.
  • The site lets you set an email alert for a town or a trade.
