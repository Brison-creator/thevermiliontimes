Vermilion Parish Election Results: Early Votes Favor Landry for DA, Broussard in Erath

Live, unofficial results from the Secretary of State as precincts report Tuesday night. Early and absentee ballots are in; most Election Day precincts are still being counted.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 3, 2026, 8:41 p.m.

8:40 p.m. update. The first numbers out of Vermilion Parish are the early and absentee ballots, and they show early leaders in most races. These are unofficial and will change as the parish’s 66 precincts report.

The races to watch

  • District Attorney, 15th Judicial District: In Vermilion’s early vote, Donald “Don” Landry leads Kristian Earles 66% to 34%. Across all three parishes in the district (Lafayette, Acadia and Vermilion), the race is much closer: Landry 50.7%, Earles 49.3%, with 1 of 244 precincts in.
  • Mayor of Erath: Stephanie P. Broussard leads Marlene Menard 159 to 58 in early votes.
  • Mayor of Gueydan: a three-way race. Efrem “Zim” Livingston has 20 early votes, Jason Suire 16 and Jeffrey Lamson 13. If nobody tops 50%, the top two go to a runoff.
  • Erath police chief: Randall Toups 134, Anna Lapointe 83.
  • Maurice police chief: tied at 128 each between John Dayries and Guy Nerren.
  • Abbeville City Council, District A: William “Billy” Noegel 166, Cornelius Darby 85.
  • Kaplan city marshal: William J. Seaux 396, Ruby Thibeaux 283.
  • School Board District C: Coeyn Wise 195, Harry “Gregg” Meaux 180. District F: Roderick Darby 301, Tyrone Alexander 172.
  • Tax renewals: Consolidated Gravity Drainage District No. 1’s 16-mill renewal and Hospital Service District No. 3’s 8.29-mill renewal are both passing about 2 to 1 in early votes.

The full results for every race on Vermilion Parish ballots, including the U.S. Senate and House races and all 10 constitutional amendments, are below and update through the night.
