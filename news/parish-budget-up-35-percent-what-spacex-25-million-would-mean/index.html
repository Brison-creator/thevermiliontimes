The Parish Budget Jumped 35% This Year, to $43.6 Million. Here’s How SpaceX’s $25 Million a Year Compares

The Police Jury planned to spend $11.4 million more in 2026 than in 2025, its own records show. All the property tax it collects in a year comes to less than $6 million.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 26, 2026, 1:30 p.m.

How big is the parish’s budget, and how much would SpaceX’s money change it? We pulled the Police Jury’s own numbers.

The budget

Each December the parish administrator sends jurors a one-page budget message with the totals. Here are the last two.

  • 2025 budget: $32,262,569 in spending, against $34,348,862 in expected revenue.
  • 2026 budget: $43,630,027 in spending, against $40,644,641 in expected revenue.

That is 35% more spending in one year, an increase of about $11.4 million. Expected revenue rose 18%.

The 2026 budget plans to spend about $3 million more than the parish expects to take in. It can do that because of what it has saved: the parish started the year with $62.6 million on hand and expects to end it with $59.6 million.

Why it went up

The posted records do not say. The budget message describes the 2026 budget as “a continuation of service levels.” The fund-by-fund pages that would show where the extra $11.4 million goes are not attached to the minutes the parish puts online.

One thing the records do show: what the parish actually spends can run well past what it budgets in December, because grants and construction projects get added during the year. In 2024, the audit shows, the Police Jury spent $53.4 million. Of that, $23.5 million was for construction and equipment, much of it paid for with federal and state money.

Where the parish’s money comes from

From the 2024 audit, the most recent one on file:

  • State money: $16.7 million
  • Sales taxes: $15.4 million
  • Federal grants: $12.2 million
  • Property taxes: $5.9 million
  • Total revenue: $55.8 million

Property tax is the smallest of the four.

Where SpaceX fits

Under the deal the state announced, SpaceX will not pay regular property taxes on the Pecan Island site. In their place it agreed to pay local taxing bodies $20 million up front and at least $25 million a year for 25 years, according to the state’s economic development agency.

For scale:

  • $25 million is more than four times all the property tax the Police Jury collected in 2024.
  • It is about 62% of everything the Police Jury expects to take in this year.

Two cautions. That money is shared among all of the parish’s taxing bodies, including the School Board and the Sheriff’s Office, not just the Police Jury, and the state’s page does not give the split. And the comparison only counts what comes in. The Gulf States Newsroom has valued the property tax break at close to $23.8 billion over 25 years, against about $800 million in payments. Residents have sued to undo the deal, saying it was approved in violation of the state’s Open Meetings Law. [Our earlier story on the lawsuit is here.](/news/residents-sue-to-void-spacex-tax-deal/)

What we don’t know yet

  • The 2027 budget. Jurors were scheduled to adopt it Nov. 18. The parish has not posted the minutes or the total.
  • The split. How much of SpaceX’s payments goes to each taxing body.
  • The reason for this year’s jump. The detailed budget is a public record. We will report what it shows when we have it.
