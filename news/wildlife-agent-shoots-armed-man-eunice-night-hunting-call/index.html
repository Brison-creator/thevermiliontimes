Wildlife Agent Shoots Man Who Pointed Gun at Him on Eunice Night-Hunting Call, State Police Say

Louisiana State Police detectives are investigating Saturday night’s shooting on Chataignier Street. The man was taken to a hospital; no agents were hurt.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 17, 2026, 6:35 a.m.

A Louisiana Department of Wildlife and Fisheries agent shot a man in Eunice on Saturday night after the man came out of a house with a gun and pointed it at the agent, according to Louisiana State Police.

It happened shortly after 8 p.m. on Saturday, Nov. 15, on Chataignier Street in Eunice, about 40 miles north of Abbeville.

Wildlife and Fisheries agents had gone to the home about a night-hunting complaint, State Police said. When they got there, a man walked out of the house armed with a firearm and aimed it at one of the agents, according to the agency’s preliminary findings. An agent fired, hitting him.

The man was taken to a hospital for treatment. State Police did not release his name or his condition, and no charges have been announced. No agents were injured.

At Wildlife and Fisheries’ request, detectives from the State Police Criminal Investigations Division’s Breaux Bridge field office are investigating. State Police called it an active investigation and said more information will come out later.

Have information? State Police are asking anyone with information, photos or video to contact detectives. Anonymous tips can go through the State Police website’s online report form, or by phone to 1-800-434-8007.
