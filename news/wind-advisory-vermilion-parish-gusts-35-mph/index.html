Wind Advisory for Vermilion Parish Until Noon Monday: Gusts Up to 35 mph

The National Weather Service warns northeast winds could knock down tree limbs, cause a few power outages and make driving tough for trucks and trailers. Tie down what’s loose tonight.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 15, 2026, 3:55 p.m.

Hold on to your hats, and your trash cans.

The National Weather Service in Lake Charles has put all of Vermilion Parish under a Wind Advisory until noon Monday, Nov. 16. The advisory covers both Upper and Lower Vermilion, along with neighboring parishes from Cameron to Iberia.

What to expect

  • Northeast winds of 15 to 20 mph, with gusts up to 35 mph, according to the advisory.
  • Loose objects can blow around, and tree limbs could come down, causing a few power outages, the weather service says.
  • Driving could be difficult, especially for high-profile vehicles like trucks, campers and trailers.

What to do tonight

  • Bring in or tie down patio furniture, trash cans, decorations and anything else that could fly.
  • If you’re towing or driving something tall on LA 82, LA 14 or US 167, slow down and keep both hands on the wheel.
  • Charge your phone in case the power goes out.

If you lose power, report it to your electric provider. Send us photos of any wind damage, and tell us who took them, at news@thevermiliontimes.com.
