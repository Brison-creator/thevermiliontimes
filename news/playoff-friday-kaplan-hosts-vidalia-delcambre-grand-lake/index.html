Playoff Friday: Kaplan Hosts Vidalia, Delcambre Goes to Grand Lake

Two Vermilion Parish teams are still alive in the LHSAA football playoffs. Both play in the regional round Friday night.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 18, 2026, 6:30 a.m.

Vermilion Parish has two football teams left, and both play Friday night.

Kaplan vs. Vidalia

No. 4 Kaplan opens its playoff run at home against No. 13 Vidalia in the regional round of Division III Non-Select, according to the Louisiana High School Athletic Association’s bracket. Kaplan sat out the first round with a bye. Vidalia got here by beating No. 20 Lakeside 28-17.

The LHSAA bracket had not posted a kickoff time for the game as of Wednesday morning. Vidalia’s hometown paper, The Natchez Democrat, reports it as 7 p.m. Friday in Kaplan.

The winner moves on to the quarterfinals against the winner of No. 5 Marksville and No. 12 Green Oaks.

Delcambre at Grand Lake

No. 25 Delcambre, which knocked off No. 8 South Plaquemines by a point last week, is on the road again. The bracket lists Delcambre at No. 9 Grand Lake at 7 p.m. Friday in Division IV Non-Select. Grand Lake beat No. 24 LaSalle 34-28 in the first round.

Scores Saturday morning

The Times will post both scores Saturday morning. [Last week’s playoff results are here.](/news/playoff-scores-delcambre-upsets-south-plaquemines/)
