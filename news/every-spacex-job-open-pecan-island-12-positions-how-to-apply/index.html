Every SpaceX Job Open at Pecan Island Right Now: What Each One Requires and How to Apply

Eleven jobs as of Nov. 24. One needs only a high school diploma. Four start in Texas and move here in early 2027. None lists pay, and no welder, electrician, operator or laborer jobs are posted yet.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 21, 2026, 6:30 p.m. · Updated Nov. 24, 2026

SpaceX had 12 jobs posted for Pecan Island when we first ran this list on Saturday, Nov. 21. Eleven remain. Here is every one, what it asks for and where to apply.

Update, Nov. 24: SpaceX has taken the Sr. Physical Security Specialist job off its board. It had been posted on Nov. 18. The company did not say why; a listing can come down because the job was filled or because it was withdrawn.

Update, Nov. 23: SpaceX’s Starbase, LA page also takes resumes by email at starbaselajobs@spacex.com, whether or not a job below fits you. [Details here.](/news/spacex-taking-resumes-starbase-louisiana-email-supplier-signup/)

Three things to know first:

  • No pay is listed on any of the postings.
  • No craft jobs are posted yet. There are no openings for welders, electricians, equipment operators, pipefitters or laborers at Pecan Island on SpaceX’s board. The jobs so far are for the managers, engineers and security staff who come before construction.
  • We check the board every day and will report new postings as they appear.

A job that needs a high school diploma

Sr. Physical Security Hardware Specialist (posted Nov. 17) High school diploma or equivalent, plus 5 or more years working with security systems such as cameras, door access, alarms or fire systems. Needs a driver’s license and comfort working at heights up to 300 feet with safety gear. Apply

Sr. Physical Security Specialist (posted Nov. 18, removed by Nov. 24) No longer listed. It asked for a high school diploma or equivalent plus 8 or more years in the military, law enforcement, overseas security contracting or corporate security.

Construction and site jobs

Sr. Construction Project Manager (posted Sept. 16) 8 or more years in engineering or construction management. Starts at Starbase, Texas, and moves to Louisiana in early 2027. Travel up to 75%. Apply

Civil Engineer, Land Development (posted Sept. 17) Engineering degree, 1 or more years of civil design work and experience with CAD software. Starts in Texas and moves to Louisiana in early 2027. Apply

Sr. Construction Safety Engineer (posted Oct. 15) Bachelor’s degree and 5 or more years running safety programs, with a focus on construction. Outdoor work on construction sites. Apply

Director, Materials Management (posted Nov. 10) Bachelor’s degree, 5 or more years in manufacturing, operations, materials or supply chain, and 4 or more years leading managers. Starts in Texas and moves to Louisiana in early 2027. Apply

Safety jobs

Sr. Manager, Environmental Health & Safety (posted Oct. 15) Bachelor’s degree, 10 or more years in safety and environmental programs and 5 or more years managing them. Must live in or move to the Pecan Island area. Apply

Manager, Environmental Health & Safety (posted Nov. 13) Bachelor’s degree, 5 or more years in safety and environmental programs and 3 or more years managing them. The job is listed at Pecan Island, though one line of the posting refers to the Cape Canaveral, Florida, area. Apply

Environmental and permit jobs

Sr. Environmental Engineer (posted Oct. 15) Bachelor’s degree and 5 or more years running environmental compliance programs. Must live in or move to the Pecan Island area. Apply

Sr. Environmental Engineer, Wetlands & Regulatory Compliance (posted Nov. 6) Bachelor’s degree and 5 or more years in environmental consulting, engineering or regulatory review. Starts in Texas and moves to Louisiana in early 2027. Apply

Sr. Environmental Regulatory Engineer, Air Permitting (posted Nov. 6) Environmental or chemical engineering degree and 7 or more years getting major air permits for power plants or industrial sites. Based at Starbase, Texas; Bastrop, Texas; or Pecan Island. Apply

Communications

Communications Lead (posted Nov. 2) Bachelor’s degree and 5 or more years in communications, events or content. On site at Pecan Island full time. Apply

Who can apply

Every posting says that, under federal export rules, an applicant must be a U.S. citizen or national, a permanent resident with a green card, a refugee or an asylee, or be eligible for the required federal authorization.

None of the jobs is remote. Several say to expect long hours and weekends.

To see the list yourself, go to SpaceX’s careers page and search for “Pecan Island.”
