Ochsner Says Hackers Got Into Two Employee Accounts and Downloaded Files That May Include Social Security Numbers and Health Information

The hospital system, which runs Ochsner Abrom Kaplan Memorial Hospital in Kaplan, began notifying people Thursday. It says patient care and its medical records system were not disrupted, and it is still working out whose information was taken.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Nov. 21, 2026, 3:20 p.m.

Ochsner Health says someone broke into two of its employees’ accounts in September and downloaded files that may contain patients’ names, Social Security numbers and health information.

The hospital system runs Ochsner Abrom Kaplan Memorial Hospital in Kaplan and Ochsner Lafayette General Medical Center in Lafayette. It posted a notice on its website and began contacting people on Thursday.

What Ochsner says happened

  • Sept. 18 to 21: An outsider got into two employee user accounts. Files in one of the accounts were downloaded.
  • Oct. 1: Ochsner learned of it, locked down the accounts and brought in an outside forensic firm, the notice says.
  • Nov. 18: Its review first confirmed that specific information had been accessed.
  • Nov. 19: Notifications began.

Ochsner says the break-in was limited to its Microsoft environment, the email and office software side of the organization. It says there was no disruption to patient care, to its electronic health record system, Epic, or to the MyOchsner patient portal.

What may have been taken

The notice says the files vary from person to person and “may include names, demographic information, Social Security numbers, and health information.”

Ochsner has not said how many people are affected, or which of its hospitals and clinics they used. It says its review is still going on to determine the full scope.

What to do

  • Watch your mail. Ochsner says it will mail letters to the people whose information was involved, with formal notice coming over the next few weeks.
  • Credit monitoring. People whose Social Security numbers were involved will be offered one year of it at no charge.
  • Questions: Ochsner has set up a phone line at 844-971-8661, open weekdays from 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Central, except major holidays.

Be careful with anyone who contacts you claiming to be from Ochsner about this. The notice says letters are coming by mail. If a caller or an email asks for your Social Security number or a payment, hang up and call the number above.

Ochsner says it has added to its cybersecurity safeguards since the incident.
