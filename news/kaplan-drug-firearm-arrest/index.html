Kaplan Man Arrested on Drug and Firearm Charges After Search of Home

Investigators seized suspected methamphetamine, marijuana, prescription pills and two guns with an estimated street value of more than $68,000, KLFY reported.

By The Vermilion Times · Published by Brison McPhail
Oct. 31, 2026, 5:48 a.m.

Police arrested a 43-year-old Kaplan man on drug and gun charges after neighbors’ complaints led investigators to a house on West Second Street, KLFY reported Thursday.

Officers said repeated complaints about suspected drug dealing at the home prompted weeks of surveillance and undercover work, followed by a search warrant, according to the station.

What was seized

Investigators listed the following, according to the report:

  • Suspected methamphetamine: about 308 grams
  • Marijuana: 171 grams, plus 40 grams of synthetic marijuana
  • Prescription drugs: 170 cyclobenzaprine tablets, 11 clonazepam tablets, 11 Adderall tablets and half a Xanax tablet
  • Suboxone: 17 strips
  • Also: two guns and drug paraphernalia

Authorities put the street value of the drugs at $68,055. Bond had not been set when the information was released, and the case is still under investigation, KLFY reported.

Charges are accusations, not convictions, and everyone arrested is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court. The Times names people arrested only when the arresting agency releases the name and charges directly; KLFY’s report did not say which agency made the arrest.
