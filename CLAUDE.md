# The Vermilion Times

Official tagline, used everywhere: **Starbase Louisiana's Local News** (`TAGLINE` in build.py).

Static newspaper site for Vermilion Parish, Louisiana, served by GitHub Pages
from `main` at https://thevermiliontimes.com. Everything is generated by
`build.py` (Python 3 standard library only). Never hand-edit generated HTML;
edit `build.py`, `assets/` or `articles/` and run `python3 build.py`.

## Adding a story

1. Create `articles/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md` (date = publication date, Central time).
2. Front matter, then the body:

   ```
   ---
   title: Headline in Title Case
   deck: One sentence that tells the reader what happened.
   section: government
   date: 2026-10-31
   sources:
   - Vermilion Parish Police Jury, meeting agenda | https://example.gov/agenda.pdf
   - KATC | https://www.katc.com/news/...
   ---
   First paragraph...

   ## Optional subhead

   More paragraphs. - list items, > quotations, **bold**, *italic*, [link](https://...).
   ```

   Sections: `local`, `government`, `safety`, `schools`, `sports`, `business`, `starbase`,
   `land-water`, `faith`, `opinion`, `obituaries`.
3. `python3 build.py` (it refuses a story with no sources or an unknown section).
4. Check the page renders (`python3 -m http.server` and open `/news/<slug>/`).
5. Commit and push to `main`.

To correct a story, edit its file, add `updated: YYYY-MM-DD`, and add a line at
the end of the body: `*Correction: ...*` saying what was wrong.

## Newsroom standards (non-negotiable)

- **Only verified facts.** Every fact must come from a source you actually opened
  and read, and every source goes in `sources:`. Prefer primary sources
  (government agendas and minutes, Secretary of State, school board, DOTD,
  LDWF, NWS, court records) plus established outlets (KATC, KLFY, KADN, The
  Advocate, Abbeville Meridional, Vermilion Today). Never rely on a search
  snippet, a social media post alone, or rumor.
- **Never invent anything:** no made-up quotes, people, numbers, dates or
  events. Quote only words that appear in a source, attribute them, and keep
  quotes short (under ~25 words). Never claim the Times interviewed anyone,
  attended anything or took a photo. Write "according to", "the agenda shows",
  "KATC reported".
- **Write it yourself.** Original wording, AP style, inverted pyramid, neutral
  tone, 300-700 words. Do not copy or closely paraphrase another outlet's
  article; report the facts and credit them.
- **Dates:** state real dates; check that an event is current and has not
  already happened (or say that it did). Today's date is in your environment.
- **Police, crime and courts (a core beat):** cover arrests, charges, wrecks,
  fires, searches and court rulings from official sources: Vermilion Parish
  Sheriff's Office, Abbeville / Kaplan / Erath / Gueydan / Delcambre police,
  Louisiana State Police (Troop I), the district attorney, court records, or an
  established outlet quoting them. Attribute every fact to the agency ("according
  to the Sheriff's Office"). Adults may be named only when the agency has
  publicly released their name and charges; say they were "arrested on" or
  "booked on" charges and that charges are accusations, not convictions. Never
  name juveniles, victims, or anyone not charged. No mugshots, no rumor, no
  social-media-only claims. If charges are dropped or a person is acquitted and
  we named them, publish that too.
- **Conflict of interest:** the publisher, Brison McPhail, owns Rocket Farm, an RV park in
  Kaplan. A standing disclosure lives on the About and Starbase Watch pages.
  Any story that touches Rocket Farm, RV parks, worker or temporary housing,
  or the publisher himself must carry `disclosure:` in the front matter:
  "Disclosure: Brison McPhail, publisher of The Vermilion Times, also owns
  Rocket Farm, an RV park in development in Kaplan." Routine Starbase stories
  do not need it. Never write promotional copy for the publisher's businesses.
- **Nothing private in the repo.** It is public: no unpublished drafts, tips,
  tipsters' names or contact details, and no email addresses other than
  news@thevermiliontimes.com (it forwards to the publisher; never publish
  his personal address).
- **If you cannot verify a story, do not publish it.** A day with no story is
  better than a wrong one.
- **Images.** Every story shows a real photo. Without its own `image:`, a story
  gets a credited file photo from its section's pool in `img/photos/pool.json`
  (captioned "File photo:"); engravings in `img/cuts/` are only a last resort.
  Grow the pools when you find good free photos (Commons rate-limits hard:
  space requests out, use 1280px thumbnails). Find photos with `python3 tools/commons.py`
  (Wikimedia Commons; public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA only). The tool
  refuses other licenses and prints the `image:`/`image_credit:`/`image_source:`
  lines; write `image_alt:` and `image_caption:` yourself, describing only what
  the photo actually shows, and never imply a file photo was taken at the
  event. U.S. government photos (NASA, NOAA, FAA, USGS, FEMA) are public domain
  and fine with credit. Never copy photos from news sites, Facebook or Google
  Images, and never use AI-generated pictures presented as photos. Look at a
  photo before using it.
- **Sections:** `safety` is Public Safety (sheriff, police, courts, fires, wrecks).
- Do not put model names or AI product identifiers in articles or commits.
- **Bylines:** compiled stories run "By The Vermilion Times" with "Published by
  Brison McPhail" (automatic). Put `byline: Name` in the front matter only for
  a story that person actually reported.

## Daily story routine

The goal is to be the paper of record for Vermilion Parish: fast, complete and
trusted. Each run publishes **up to three** verified stories (one is fine on a
slow day; zero if nothing can be verified).

1. `git pull`, then list `articles/` so you do not repeat a story already run.
   Follow-ups with genuinely new facts are welcome.
2. Sweep the feelers: `python3 tools/gnews.py` runs every query below and prints
   each item's real publisher URL (Google's links are redirects). KLFY and WWL
   often block fetches; append `amp/` to KLFY URLs, or find another outlet.
   Google News RSS also works directly with curl:
   `https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=<query>&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en`.
   Run at least these queries: `Vermilion+Parish`, `Starbase+Louisiana`,
   `SpaceX+Pecan+Island`, `Vermilion+Parish+Sheriff`, `Abbeville+Louisiana`,
   `Kaplan+Louisiana`, `Erath+Louisiana`, `Gueydan`, `Delcambre`,
   `Maurice+Louisiana`, `Intracoastal+City`. Then check primary sources
   directly: vppj.org (agendas, minutes, notices), voterportal.sos.la.gov,
   the School Board, City of Abbeville, Town of Kaplan, DOTD, LDWF, NWS Lake
   Charles, FAA (Starbase Louisiana environmental review), Louisiana Economic
   Development.
3. Beats, in priority order:
   - **Starbase Louisiana / SpaceX**: every development (land, lawsuits, FAA,
     public meetings, roads, jobs, the tax deal, launches). Disclose when housing or RV parks come up.
   - **Police and public safety** (rules above).
   - **Government**: Police Jury, councils, School Board, elections, taxes.
   - Weather and the coast, roads, schools, sports, business, community.
4. Open and read every source you use (search snippets are not sources; if a
   page is blocked, find another outlet or the primary record). Write each
   story, build, check the pages render, commit (`Add story: <headline>`) and
   push to `main`.
