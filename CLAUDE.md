# The Vermilion Times

Static newspaper site for Vermilion Parish, Louisiana, served by GitHub Pages
from `main` at https://thevermiliontimes.com. Everything is generated by
`build.py` (Python 3 standard library only). Never hand-edit generated HTML;
edit `build.py`, `assets/` or `articles/` and run `python3 build.py`.

## Adding a story

1. Create `articles/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md` (date = publication date, Central time).
2. Front matter, then the body:

   ```
   ---
   title: Headline in Title Case
   deck: One sentence that tells the reader what happened.
   section: government
   date: 2026-10-31
   sources:
   - Vermilion Parish Police Jury, meeting agenda | https://example.gov/agenda.pdf
   - KATC | https://www.katc.com/news/...
   ---
   First paragraph...

   ## Optional subhead

   More paragraphs. - list items, > quotations, **bold**, *italic*, [link](https://...).
   ```

   Sections: `local`, `government`, `schools`, `sports`, `business`, `starbase`,
   `land-water`, `faith`, `opinion`, `obituaries`.
3. `python3 build.py` (it refuses a story with no sources or an unknown section).
4. Check the page renders (`python3 -m http.server` and open `/news/<slug>/`).
5. Commit and push to `main`.

To correct a story, edit its file, add `updated: YYYY-MM-DD`, and add a line at
the end of the body: `*Correction: ...*` saying what was wrong.

## Newsroom standards (non-negotiable)

- **Only verified facts.** Every fact must come from a source you actually opened
  and read, and every source goes in `sources:`. Prefer primary sources
  (government agendas and minutes, Secretary of State, school board, DOTD,
  LDWF, NWS, court records) plus established outlets (KATC, KLFY, KADN, The
  Advocate, Abbeville Meridional, Vermilion Today). Never rely on a search
  snippet, a social media post alone, or rumor.
- **Never invent anything:** no made-up quotes, people, numbers, dates or
  events. Quote only words that appear in a source, attribute them, and keep
  quotes short (under ~25 words). Never claim the Times interviewed anyone,
  attended anything or took a photo. Write "according to", "the agenda shows",
  "KATC reported".
- **Write it yourself.** Original wording, AP style, inverted pyramid, neutral
  tone, 300-700 words. Do not copy or closely paraphrase another outlet's
  article; report the facts and credit them.
- **Dates:** state real dates; check that an event is current and has not
  already happened (or say that it did). Today's date is in your environment.
- **Crime and courts:** do not name private individuals who are suspects or
  arrested unless formally charged per an official record, and even then
  prefer stories that do not need names. No mugshots, no speculation.
- **Conflict of interest:** the publisher owns Rocket Farm, an RV park in
  Kaplan. Any story touching Rocket Farm, RV parks or worker housing near
  Starbase Louisiana, or the publisher must carry `disclosure:` in the front
  matter saying so. Never write promotional copy for the publisher's businesses.
- **Nothing private in the repo.** It is public: no unpublished drafts, tips,
  tipsters' names or contact details, and no email addresses other than
  news@thevermiliontimes.com.
- **If you cannot verify a story, do not publish it.** A day with no story is
  better than a wrong one.
- Do not invent images. Stories run as text.
- Do not put model names or AI product identifiers in articles or commits.

## Daily story routine

1. `git pull`, then list `articles/` so you do not repeat a story already run.
2. Search for news from the last 48 hours (or events in the next 7 days) in
   Vermilion Parish: Abbeville, Kaplan, Erath, Gueydan, Delcambre, Maurice,
   Pecan Island, Intracoastal City and the coast. Good beats: Police Jury, city
   and town councils, school board, elections, roads and DOTD, weather and the
   coast, fishing and hunting seasons, rice/crawfish/cattle, school sports,
   festivals, church and community events, business openings, Starbase
   Louisiana at Pecan Island.
3. Pick the one story with the best sources and broadest local interest. A
   follow-up to an earlier story is fine if there is something new.
4. Write it, build, check, commit (`Add story: <headline>`) and push to `main`.
