Starbase Watch

A running record of SpaceX’s planned Starbase Louisiana launch site near Pecan Island: what has been decided, by whom, what it is worth, and what is still unanswered. Every line links to its source. Last updated Oct. 31, 2026.

Start here: [Where is Starbase Louisiana?](/news/where-is-starbase-louisiana/) · [SpaceX Louisiana jobs: every opening so far](/news/spacex-louisiana-jobs-pecan-island/) · [What the parish knows so far](/news/starbase-louisiana-what-we-know/)

By the numbers

  • About 125,000 acres near Pecan Island in Vermilion Parish, according to The Center Square.
  • 3,000 direct jobs expected over the next decade, with an average salary of $92,600, according to The Center Square. The tax agreement describes local hiring as an aspiration, not a requirement, Acadiana News First reported.
  • $25 million a year in payments to the parish to start, rising 2% a year for 25 years, about $800 million in all by the Gulf States Newsroom’s calculation, according to WWNO.
  • Nearly $23.8 billion in property tax exemptions over 25 years, per the Gulf States Newsroom; The Center Square puts the full state and local incentive package at about $27.5 billion.
  • Up to $600,000 a year in administrative fees to the parish Industrial Development Board, according to Acadiana News First.
  • 8 SpaceX job openings listed at Pecan Island on the company’s job board as of Oct. 31, 2026, all senior engineering, construction, safety and community roles. [See the list.](/news/spacex-louisiana-jobs-pecan-island/)
  • 2027 construction expected to begin; 2029 first launch targeted, according to The Center Square.

Timeline

  • July 15, 2026: The Vermilion Parish Police Jury amends its agenda during the meeting to revive the parish Industrial Development Board, according to the lawsuit described by WWNO.
  • July 23, 2026: The Industrial Development Board meets. Recordings were later obtained by Vermilion United.
  • Aug. 25, 2026: The development board approves the payment-in-lieu-of-taxes agreement. The agenda used the code name “Project Osprey,” WWNO reported.
  • Sept. 16, 2026: SpaceX posts its first job listed at Pecan Island, a senior construction project manager, according to the company’s job board.
  • Oct. 26, 2026: The Vermilion Citizens Coalition and three residents sue the Police Jury and the development board under the Open Meetings Law, asking a judge to void the Aug. 25 approval. [Our story.](/news/residents-sue-to-void-spacex-tax-deal/)
  • Oct. 28, 2026: Acadiana News First reports the school district owns about 4,000 acres of Section 16 school land near White Lake, next to the site, which would be appraised before any swap. Gov. Jeff Landry’s former chief of staff, Kyle Ruckert, is reported registered as a SpaceX lobbyist.
  • Oct. 30, 2026: The Advocate reports the School Board voted to have the 4,000 acres appraised as a first step toward talks with SpaceX; the land now earns $20,000 to $30,000 a year in hunting leases. [Our story](/news/school-board-spacex-land-appraisal/).
  • Oct. 29, 2026: Vermilion United questions local hiring, the $600,000 fee and an unwritten fire-protection pledge. [Our story.](/news/vermilion-united-questions-spacex-deal/)
  • Oct. 31, 2026: The Center Square reports DOTD selected Royal Engineers & Consultants, a firm that gave $115,000 to a Landry-aligned PAC, for work tied to the site; the state is evaluating LA 82 improvements without having settled who pays. [Our story.](/news/landry-donor-firm-spacex-dotd-contract/)
  • Dec. 14, 2026: The earliest date Louisiana Economic Development told Vermilion United it would release the state’s cooperative endeavor agreement with SpaceX, according to the group.

Still unanswered

  • Will any of the 3,000 jobs be guaranteed to Vermilion Parish residents?
  • What does the state’s cooperative endeavor agreement with SpaceX say? It is not public.
  • Will SpaceX’s verbal promise of fire equipment, meant to protect homeowners’ insurance rates, be put in writing?
  • Where will the development board’s annual administrative fee go?
  • Who pays for upgrades to LA 82 and other roads: taxpayers or SpaceX? Was DOTD’s SpaceX-related engineering contract bid out?
  • What is the school land near White Lake worth, and what would the parish’s schools get in a swap?
  • How will a judge rule on the open meetings lawsuit, and when?
