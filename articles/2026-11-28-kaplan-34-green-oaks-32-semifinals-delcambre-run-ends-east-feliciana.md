Kaplan is going to the state semifinals. It took everything the Pirates had.

## Kaplan 34, Green Oaks 32

**No. 4 Kaplan** held off **No. 12 Green Oaks 34-32** at home Friday night in the Division III Non-Select quarterfinals, according to the Louisiana High School Athletic Association's bracket.

It was nothing like last week, when Kaplan beat Vidalia 56-6. Green Oaks, from Shreveport, had already knocked out No. 5 Marksville and came within two points of doing it again.

**Next:** the top seed. Kaplan goes to **No. 1 Jena** for the semifinals. Jena beat No. 8 Church Point 34-23 on Friday. The LHSAA lists the semifinals for Dec. 3 and 4. It had not posted a day or time for Kaplan's game as of late Friday.

The winner plays for the state championship at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans, Dec. 10 to 12.

## East Feliciana 56, Delcambre 16

The road finally ran out for **No. 25 Delcambre**, which lost **56-16** at **No. 1 East Feliciana** in the Division IV Non-Select quarterfinals.

It ends one of the best stories of the playoffs. Delcambre was seeded 25th, went on the road every week, beat No. 8 South Plaquemines 19-18 and then beat No. 9 Grand Lake 41-15. That made the Panthers one of the last eight teams standing in their division.

## How Kaplan got here

- **First round:** bye
- **Second round:** Kaplan 56, Vidalia 6
- **Quarterfinals:** Kaplan 34, Green Oaks 32
- **Semifinals:** at No. 1 Jena, Dec. 3 or 4
