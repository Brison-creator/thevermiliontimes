SpaceX has 12 jobs posted for Pecan Island as of Saturday. Here is every one, what it asks for and where to apply.

Three things to know first:

- **No pay is listed** on any of the 12 postings.
- **No craft jobs are posted yet.** There are no openings for welders, electricians, equipment operators, pipefitters or laborers at Pecan Island on SpaceX's board. The jobs so far are for the managers, engineers and security staff who come before construction.
- **We check the board every day** and will report new postings as they appear.

## Jobs that need a high school diploma

**Sr. Physical Security Hardware Specialist** (posted Nov. 17)
High school diploma or equivalent, plus 5 or more years working with security systems such as cameras, door access, alarms or fire systems. Needs a driver's license and comfort working at heights up to 300 feet with safety gear. [Apply](https://boards.greenhouse.io/spacex/jobs/8943802002?gh_jid=8943802002)

**Sr. Physical Security Specialist** (posted Nov. 18)
High school diploma or equivalent, plus 8 or more years in the military, law enforcement, overseas security contracting or corporate security, and 2 or more years in executive protection or emergency response. Travel up to 30% of the time. [Apply](https://boards.greenhouse.io/spacex/jobs/8950926002?gh_jid=8950926002)

## Construction and site jobs

**Sr. Construction Project Manager** (posted Sept. 16)
8 or more years in engineering or construction management. Starts at Starbase, Texas, and moves to Louisiana in early 2027. Travel up to 75%. [Apply](https://boards.greenhouse.io/spacex/jobs/8814705002?gh_jid=8814705002)

**Civil Engineer, Land Development** (posted Sept. 17)
Engineering degree, 1 or more years of civil design work and experience with CAD software. Starts in Texas and moves to Louisiana in early 2027. [Apply](https://boards.greenhouse.io/spacex/jobs/8814619002?gh_jid=8814619002)

**Sr. Construction Safety Engineer** (posted Oct. 15)
Bachelor's degree and 5 or more years running safety programs, with a focus on construction. Outdoor work on construction sites. [Apply](https://boards.greenhouse.io/spacex/jobs/8882178002?gh_jid=8882178002)

**Director, Materials Management** (posted Nov. 10)
Bachelor's degree, 5 or more years in manufacturing, operations, materials or supply chain, and 4 or more years leading managers. Starts in Texas and moves to Louisiana in early 2027. [Apply](https://boards.greenhouse.io/spacex/jobs/8936748002?gh_jid=8936748002)

## Communications

**Communications Lead** (posted Nov. 2)
Bachelor's degree and 5 or more years in communications, events or content. On site at Pecan Island full time. [Apply](https://boards.greenhouse.io/spacex/jobs/8926138002?gh_jid=8926138002)

## Who can apply

Every posting says that, under federal export rules, an applicant must be a U.S. citizen or national, a permanent resident with a green card, a refugee or an asylee, or be eligible for the required federal authorization.

None of the jobs is remote. Several say to expect long hours and weekends.

To see the list yourself, go to SpaceX's careers page and search for "Pecan Island."
