Alaska has a new U.S. senator. Democrat **Mary Peltola** has beaten Republican incumbent **Dan Sullivan**, according to results posted Tuesday evening by the Alaska Division of Elections.

With all 401 precincts counted, the state's summary report shows:

- **Mary Peltola (D): 144,302 votes, 50.12%**
- **Dan S. Sullivan (R): 131,844 votes, 45.80%**
- Gerald L. Heikes (R): 6,105 votes, 2.12%
- Daniel J. Sullivan Jr.: 4,760 votes, 1.65%
- Write-ins: 885 votes, 0.31%

The Associated Press called the race for Peltola.

## Why 50% matters

Alaska uses ranked-choice voting, where voters can rank the candidates in order. If nobody gets more than half of the first-choice votes, the last-place candidate is dropped and those ballots move to the voters' next choice, round by round. Peltola's 50.12% clears that bar on first choices alone.

The margin was about 12,500 votes out of nearly 288,000 cast in the race. Turnout was 47.9% of Alaska's 605,433 registered voters.

## Who they are

Peltola served in the U.S. House from 2022 to 2025 and was the first Alaska Native elected to Congress. Sullivan has held the Senate seat since 2015.

The state labels the results unofficial until they are certified.
