Both Vermilion Parish teams still playing will kick off at the same time Friday night, the day after Thanksgiving.

The LHSAA bracket now lists Delcambre's game, which had been missing a date and site since the weekend.

## Kaplan vs. Green Oaks

- **When:** Friday, Nov. 27, 7 p.m.
- **Where:** Kaplan
- **Seeds:** Kaplan is No. 4. Green Oaks is No. 12.
- **How they got here:** Kaplan had a first-round bye, then beat Vidalia 56-6. Green Oaks beat Donaldsonville 32-14 and then won at No. 5 Marksville, 30-26.

## Delcambre at East Feliciana

- **When:** Friday, Nov. 27, 7 p.m.
- **Where:** East Feliciana
- **Seeds:** Delcambre is No. 25. East Feliciana is No. 1.
- **How they got here:** Delcambre has won twice on the road, 19-18 at No. 8 South Plaquemines and 41-15 at No. 9 Grand Lake. East Feliciana had a bye, then beat West St. Mary 38-12.

A No. 25 seed in the quarterfinals has already beaten two teams it was not supposed to. This is the third.

## Bring a jacket

The National Weather Service forecast for Kaplan:

- **Tonight (Tuesday):** Rain, a 70% chance, as a front comes through.
- **Wednesday:** Sunny, high near 55. Low near 38.
- **Thanksgiving:** Sunny, high near 57. Low near 38.
- **Friday:** Partly sunny, high near 59. No rain expected for the game, with temperatures falling toward a low near 42 overnight and almost no wind.

## What's next

Friday's winners play in the semifinals Dec. 3 or 4. The championship games are Dec. 10 through 12 at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans.
