Five Vermilion Parish football teams are in the state playoffs, according to the brackets the Louisiana High School Athletic Association released Sunday. Kaplan has the best seed of the group.

## The matchups

**Kaplan, Division III Non-Select, No. 4 seed:** first-round **bye**. Kaplan will host the winner of No. 13 Vidalia and No. 20 Lakeside in the regional round, set for Nov. 19-21.

**Erath, Division II Non-Select, No. 20 seed:** at No. 13 **Northwest** in the bi-district round, Nov. 13-15.

**North Vermilion, Division II Non-Select, No. 26 seed:** at No. 7 **Lutcher**, Nov. 13-15.

**Delcambre, Division IV Non-Select, No. 25 seed:** at No. 8 **South Plaquemines**, Nov. 12-14.

**Vermilion Catholic, Division IV Select, No. 22 seed:** at No. 11 **St. John**, Nov. 12-14.

The LHSAA lists date ranges for each round; schools set the exact kickoff times. Abbeville and Gueydan do not appear in this year's brackets.

## The road to the Dome

After the bi-district round come regionals (Nov. 19-21), quarterfinals (Nov. 26-28) and semifinals (Dec. 3-4). The state championship games, the LHSAA Prep Classic, are Dec. 10-12 at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans.

The Vermilion Times will post scores for every parish team after each round. Coaches, parents and students: send us your playoff photos and we'll run the best ones.
