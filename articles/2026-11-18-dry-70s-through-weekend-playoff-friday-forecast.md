If you have yard work, a hunting trip or a football game on the calendar, the weather is on your side.

The National Weather Service forecast for Kaplan calls for **mostly sunny days in the mid-70s and nights in the upper 50s** from now through Sunday, with rain chances that never get above single digits. No weather alerts were in effect for Vermilion Parish at midday Wednesday.

## Day by day

- **Wednesday:** Mostly sunny, high near 73. Low around 55 overnight.
- **Thursday:** Mostly sunny, high near 75, with a northeast breeze of 10 to 15 mph and gusts up to 20. Low around 57.
- **Friday:** Mostly sunny, high near 75. Low around 59.
- **Saturday:** Mostly sunny, high near 77. Low around 59.
- **Sunday:** Mostly sunny, high near 78.

## Friday night football

Friday evening looks partly cloudy and dry, with light northeast wind around 5 mph and temperatures sliding toward an overnight low near 59. That's good news for the two parish teams still in the playoffs: [Kaplan hosts Vidalia and Delcambre plays at Grand Lake](/news/playoff-friday-kaplan-hosts-vidalia-delcambre-grand-lake/).

Forecasts change. Check the Weather Service's latest before you head out.
