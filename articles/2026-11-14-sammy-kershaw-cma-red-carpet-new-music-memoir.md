Kaplan's own **Sammy Kershaw** is going to the CMA Awards after all, red carpet and all.

A week after he told fans the Country Music Association had denied his request to walk the red carpet, Kershaw said he and his wife, Mendy, have accepted the CMA's invitation to walk it and attend the show. The 60th Annual CMA Awards are **Wednesday, Nov. 18**, at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, according to the CMA.

"After a lot of thought and prayer, Mendy and I have decided to accept the CMA's invitation to walk the red carpet and attend the awards," Kershaw wrote in a Nov. 12 Instagram post, as quoted by Us Weekly.

## What's next for Sammy

The Times asked Kershaw's team what his hometown should know. His manager, **Billy Holland** of The Holland Group in Nashville, answered in an email Friday.

Kershaw "is working on new music, his memoir and lots of touring," Holland told the Times.

## "YOU helped make this happen"

The dispute started Nov. 5, when Kershaw posted that the CMA had turned down his request to spend a few minutes on the red carpet, calling it "extremely hurtful, embarrassing and very disappointing." Country stars and thousands of fans spoke up for him, and his classic songs climbed back up the iTunes chart.

On Nov. 9 the CMA said it was "deeply sorry anyone felt excluded" and that it was talking with Kershaw's team. By the end of the week he had accepted.

In his Nov. 12 post, Kershaw thanked the people who backed him. "It's been a week since so many of you began making your voices heard, and the outpouring of love and support has meant more to me than you'll ever know," he wrote, as quoted by KALB.

"To the thousands of fans and fellow artists who stood beside us, please know that YOU helped make this happen," he wrote.

He said he was thinking of the legends who came before him: "I think about Dolly, Loretta, George Jones, Charley Pride, and so many others who gave their hearts, voices, and lives to country music."

## Watch for him Wednesday

The show is Wednesday night, Nov. 18, from Nashville.
