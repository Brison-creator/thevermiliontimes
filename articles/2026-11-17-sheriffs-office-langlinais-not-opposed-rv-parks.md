Vermilion Parish Sheriff Eddie Langlinais has not said RV parks will be kept out of the parish, and he doesn't oppose them, the Sheriff's Office told The Vermilion Times on Tuesday.

"Sheriff Langlinais has not made any statements that RV parks will not be allowed in Vermilion Parish, and he is not opposed to RV parks," Ashley Adams, the office's public information officer, wrote in an email.

## Why we asked

A claim that the sheriff had said there would be no RV parks in Vermilion Parish reached the Times this week. We could not confirm it, so we did not report it. We put the question to the Sheriff's Office in writing on Tuesday, and Adams answered the same afternoon.

## The sheriff doesn't issue the permits

Adams also said the question isn't the sheriff's to decide.

"The Vermilion Parish Sheriff's Office has no authority over the approval or permitting of RV parks," she wrote. "Those decisions are handled by the appropriate local governing authorities."

Asked whether the office has public safety concerns about RV parks or housing for workers as the parish grows, Adams said: "The Sheriff's Office's main concern is public safety, and we will continue to serve and protect all areas of Vermilion Parish as our communities grow."

## Where the rules are being written

Two local bodies have RV parks in front of them this fall.

- **The Police Jury** approved a parish RV ordinance on Oct. 21, on the condition that its lawyer first strike every mention of temporary housing other than RVs, according to the meeting's draft minutes. The final text had not been posted on the parish website when the Times [reported that vote](/news/police-jury-rv-ordinance-fines-levee-district/).
- **The Abbeville City Council** has a discussion and possible vote on "the establishment of new RV/Campground Parks" on [tonight's agenda](/news/abbeville-council-tonight-rv-parks-meadows-phase-ii/). The council meets at 5:30 p.m. at City Hall, 101 N. State St., and takes public comment before it votes.

The Times has also sent written questions about that agenda item to the City of Abbeville and will report what the council does.
