The Vermilion Parish Police Jury is set to adopt its 2027 budget Wednesday evening, then decide how to answer the lawsuit that seeks to undo the parish's tax deal with SpaceX.

Both meetings are in the Police Jury meeting room on the second floor of the courthouse, **100 N. State St., Suite 200, Abbeville**. The special budget meeting starts at **5:30 p.m.**, and the regular meeting follows it. Each one opens with a chance for the public to speak on anything on the agenda. The parish posted the notice Tuesday afternoon.

## The 2027 budget

The budget meeting has one piece of business: to "discuss, review and adopt the 2027 budget." The agenda doesn't give totals.

One budget-related number is on the regular agenda. Jurors will be asked to approve moving **$383,040.17** from the parish's dedicated tax funds, such as the health unit, road districts and the Ward 8 cemetery fund, into the general fund to cover their share of administrative costs. The parish's table puts the administrative salaries being split at **$499,986**.

## The SpaceX lawsuit

Two items from the Courthouse/General Needs Committee deal with the suit filed by the Vermilion Citizens Coalition and residents Cheryl Choate, Rebecca C. Young and Dr. Robert J. Young III against the Police Jury and the parish's Industrial Development Board:

- letting the parish's lawyer **respond to the lawsuit**, and
- letting the lawyer **put out a press release** about it.

The plaintiffs say the two bodies broke Louisiana's Open Meetings Law in approving the Starbase Louisiana tax agreement, as the Times [reported Oct. 30](/news/residents-sue-to-void-spacex-tax-deal/). The Police Jury declined to comment when the suit was filed, WWNO reported at the time.

## The mosquito plane

The same committee recommends approving an **emergency engine repair and purchase** for the parish's mosquito plane. The agenda doesn't list a cost.

## Coast and flooding

- A resolution letting the jury president sign the paperwork to build **Phase II of the North Shore of Vermilion Bay Shoreline Protection Project**.
- The final payment to Broussard Brothers Inc. for the **Schooner Bayou rehabilitation** project.
- A recommendation to **hold off on updating the parish's Coastal Master Plan** until jurors talk again with Royal Engineering.
- Having Sellers & Associates start the parish's application to FEMA's 2026 **Flood Mitigation Assistance** program.
- Three items on the **Chenier Au Tigre gap closure** project, including a closed session on a possible lawsuit against Great Midwest Insurance Co. and steps toward agreements with insurers to get the project finished.

## Also on the agenda

- Signing on to the **Albertsons opioid settlement**, and paying JEPCO Holdings LLC for recovery housing services under the parish's opioid program.
- A resolution backing **St. Tammany Parish** in its court fight over state rules that make parishes pay for state offices without giving them the money to do it.
- A request for 2026-27 state money from the **Local Government Assistance** and **Community Water Enrichment Fund** programs.
- A letter from the parish's lawyer about blighted property at **6912 and 6914 South Lake Cove Road**.
- A noise variance for **The Marquette** on December evenings, from 5 to 11:30 p.m.
- Two parish dumpsters for **KLFY's Food Net drive in Kaplan** on Tuesday, Dec. 15, at 1007 N. Irving.
- Training so drainage boards can do specialized burning during a burn ban.
- A pond permit on North Road in Erath and final plats for two land partitions.

The jury's next regular meeting is set for Wednesday, Dec. 16, at 5:30 p.m. The Times will report what jurors decide.
