New RV and campground parks are back in front of the Abbeville City Council tonight.

The council meets at **5:30 p.m. Tuesday at City Hall, 101 N. State St.**, and the public can comment on any agenda item before the council votes, according to the posted agenda. Here's what's on it.

## RV and campground parks

Under old business, the council has a **discussion and possible action on "the establishment of new RV/Campground Parks,"** an item put forward by Bart Broussard. The agenda doesn't say what rules or locations are being considered. The city already operates its own RV park, which shows up as a line in its monthly budget reports.

## The Meadows, Phase II

**Elray Schexnaider** is scheduled to update the council on how work is going at St. Francis Woods on **The Meadows Phase II**. In a related item, Councilman William Noegel is asking for a **variance so Glenn Lege Construction can finish burning trees** cleared at St. Francis Woods.

## Money items

- **Government Building repairs:** approval of roof and elevator repair expenses at the Government Building, **407 Charity St.**, paid out of **$145,000** the council already approved, at the request of Councilman Brady Broussard Jr.
- **Police gear:** ratifying a **$10,382** payment to Sentinel Supply for tactical gear, requested by Police Chief Mike Hardy.
- **Blight clearance:** a final payment of **$18,794.87** to Lathan Construction LLC for Phase I of the city's FY 2023 Louisiana Community Development Block Grant clearance program.
- **State aid:** a resolution asking for 2026-27 money from two state programs, **Local Government Assistance** and the **Community Water Enrichment Fund**, and another letting the mayor sign paperwork for DOTD's **Transportation Alternatives Program**.
- The council will also ratify October's bills and review the October budget report.

## Also on the agenda

- A special event permit for the first **Blinged Out Ridez** trail ride on **Dec. 13**, 8 a.m. to midnight, at The Red Barn, 2901 Rodeo Road. The permit lets riders use the city street.
- Possible action on adjudicated property at **707 and 715 Dutel St.**
- Accepting the resignation of an Abbeville Police officer.
- Reports from the city engineers, attorney, public works, parks and recreation, the mayor, and the police and fire chiefs.

The Times will report what the council decides.
