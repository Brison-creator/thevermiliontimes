Cover your tender plants tonight. The National Weather Service forecasts a low near **34 degrees** in Vermilion Parish, with patches of frost after 5 a.m. Friday and widespread frost before 8 a.m.

There is no freeze warning for the parish. The Weather Service office in Lake Charles issued one for central Louisiana, along and north of U.S. 190, from midnight to 9 a.m. Friday.

Forecasters said clouds drifting in overnight could keep it a few degrees warmer than that, mostly in the southern part of the region. Either way, they called it an unseasonably cold night, with everywhere but the immediate coast dropping into the 30s.

## Hour by hour

- **Tonight:** 48 degrees by 7 p.m., 42 by 10 p.m.
- **Friday, 5 to 7 a.m.:** 34 to 35 degrees, with frost.
- **Friday afternoon:** Mostly cloudy, high near 58.

## Friday night football

Both parish teams still playing kick off at 7 p.m. Friday, and both games will be cold and dry.

- **Green Oaks at Kaplan:** About 51 degrees at kickoff, falling to 47 by the fourth quarter. Mostly cloudy, light west wind.
- **Delcambre at East Feliciana:** The forecast for Jackson, where the school is, is about 44 degrees at kickoff and 39 by 10 p.m. Partly cloudy, nearly calm.

## Dry and breezy

The Weather Service also flagged a higher risk of grass and brush fires Thursday afternoon. Northwest winds were forecast to gust to about 25 mph, the air is very dry, and the region is in a drought. Winds ease Friday, but the air stays dry.

## The weekend

- **Saturday:** Mostly sunny and warmer, high near 69.
- **Sunday:** A cold front comes through in the morning. There is a 50% chance of showers and thunderstorms before noon, then mostly sunny with a high near 75 and wind gusts up to 30 mph. Forecasters expect less than a quarter inch of rain in most places.

Scores from both quarterfinals will be posted here Saturday morning.
