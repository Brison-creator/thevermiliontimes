If you grabbed a biscuit sandwich out of the cooler at work in the past month, check the label.

A Texas food maker has widened a recall of ready-to-eat biscuit products that were sold in Louisiana, because the biscuits in them may carry **Listeria**, a germ that can cause serious illness. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration posted the company's notice this week.

## What's recalled

**Fresh & Ready Foods LLC** of Longview, Texas, says two products made at its Longview plant are affected:

- **Fresh To You Sausage, Egg & Cheese Biscuit Sandwich**, 5.7 ounces, wrapped in clear plastic film. Item number **3100048**, with "Fresh Thru" dates from **11/12 to 11/22** on the front label.
- **Mmm! Meals Made in Minutes Biscuit & Sausage Gravy**, 9.5 ounces, in a clear clamshell. Item number **90013**, with expiration dates from **11/12 to 11/18** on the front label.

Both were shipped to **Louisiana**. The sandwich also went to Alabama, Arkansas, Mississippi, Oklahoma, Tennessee and Texas, and the gravy meal went to all of those except Alabama.

## Where they were sold

Not on grocery shelves. The company says the products went out between **Oct. 15 and Nov. 11** through **workplace break rooms, grab-and-go coolers, chilled vending machines and micro-markets**, the small self-checkout snack shops found in many workplaces.

## Why

The problem started with the biscuits. Fresh & Ready says it used biscuits from another company, C.H. Guenther & Son, which has expanded its own recall over possible Listeria contamination. Fresh & Ready first recalled some of the sandwiches on Nov. 8 and added more products on Nov. 17.

The company says **no illnesses have been reported**.

## Who is most at risk

The notice says Listeria infections can be severe, even deadly, for small children, the frail and elderly, and people whose immune systems are weak. Otherwise healthy people can be sick for a few days with a high fever, a bad headache, stiffness, nausea, stomach pain and diarrhea. In pregnant women, an infection can cause miscarriage or stillbirth.

## What to do

- **Don't eat it.** Throw away any of the recalled products you still have.
- For a **full refund** or questions, call Fresh & Ready Foods at **1-855-424-8390**, weekdays from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central.

If your workplace has one of these coolers or vending machines, pass this along to whoever stocks it.
