If you have family who moved to Houston for work, the state of Louisiana is about to ask them to move back.

Louisiana Economic Development says it will put up billboards along Interstate 10 East in Houston over the next several weeks. The message is aimed at Louisiana natives living in Texas: "It's time to come home."

## What the state is doing

- **Billboards** on I-10 East in Houston.
- **A full-page ad** in the Houston Business Journal, aimed at company owners and executives.
- **A job site.** The campaign sends job seekers to LouisianaNOW.jobs, the state's free job board.

The state calls this the first phase of a larger push into Texas to bring workers back, recruit new companies and compete for investment.

## Why now

The state's announcement ties the timing to recent elections in Texas, which it says brought "a significant shift in the state's political leadership."

"Texas, Louisiana is open for business. Come grow with us," Gov. Jeff Landry said in the announcement.

The state says nearly 60,000 new jobs have been announced across Louisiana since 2024. Those are jobs companies have promised, not jobs already filled.
