If your garbage goes out on a Thursday or Friday, you'll need to hold it a day longer during Thanksgiving week.

**Waste Connections**, the parish's garbage hauler, is shifting pickups around the holiday, according to a notice the **Vermilion Parish Police Jury** posted on its website:

- **Thursday, Nov. 26 (Thanksgiving Day):** no pickup. Thursday routes will be picked up **Friday, Nov. 27**.
- **Friday, Nov. 27:** Friday routes will be picked up **Saturday, Nov. 28**.
- **Monday, Nov. 30:** the regular schedule is back.

The notice lists no changes for Monday through Wednesday of that week.

The hauler asks residents to roll their cans to the curb **the night before** their new pickup day so they don't get missed.

If you have questions about your route, the Police Jury's office is at 337-898-4300.
