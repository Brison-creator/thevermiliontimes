If you are driving this week, it will cost less than it did at the start of the month.

The federal government's weekly fuel survey put the average price of regular gasoline on the Gulf Coast at **$3.13 a gallon** on Monday. Three weeks ago it was $3.64.

The Gulf Coast region in the survey is Louisiana, Texas, Mississippi, Alabama, Arkansas and New Mexico. It is the cheapest region in the country for gasoline.

## Gasoline

- **Gulf Coast regular: $3.13**, down 17 cents in a week and 51 cents in three weeks.
- **U.S. average: $3.64**, down 16 cents in a week and 51 cents in three weeks.
- **Compared with last year:** still higher. The Gulf Coast average is about 48 cents above this week a year ago.

## Diesel

Diesel is falling too, but it is still far above last year, and that reaches everyone who hauls, farms or runs equipment for a living.

- **Gulf Coast diesel: $5.29 a gallon**, down 25 cents in a week.
- **U.S. average: $5.70**, down 24 cents in a week.
- **Compared with last year:** Gulf Coast diesel is $1.84 a gallon higher than a year ago.

## What the numbers are

These are regional averages from the U.S. Energy Information Administration, which surveys stations every Monday. The price at any one station in Vermilion Parish can be higher or lower. The agency does not publish a number for the parish or for Louisiana alone.
