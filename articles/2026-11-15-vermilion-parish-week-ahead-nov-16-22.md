It's a full week in the parish. Here's what's coming, day by day. Save it, share it, and send us anything we missed at news@thevermiliontimes.com.

## Tuesday, Nov. 17

- **Abbeville City Council,** 5:30 p.m. The council meets the first and third Tuesday of each month, according to the city. City Hall is at 101 N. State St.
- **FAA online meetings on SpaceX's Texas plans,** 1-3 p.m. and 5:30-7:30 p.m. The FAA is taking public comment on SpaceX's request for up to 456 Starship launches a year from Starbase, Texas, with landings in the Gulf. Anyone can join online; registration is on the FAA's project page. [How to take part.](/news/faa-starbase-texas-456-launches-gulf-landings-virtual-hearings/)

## Wednesday, Nov. 18

- **Vermilion Parish Police Jury,** regular meeting. The jury meets on the third Wednesday of each month, and its last regular meeting began at 5:30 p.m. in the Police Jury meeting room on the second floor of the courthouse, 100 N. State St. The agenda is usually posted on vppj.org the day before. We'll tell you what's on it.
- **FAA online meeting,** 5:30-7:30 p.m.
- **The 60th CMA Awards** in Nashville, where Kaplan's own **Sammy Kershaw** plans to walk the red carpet with his wife, Mendy. [Read our story.](/news/sammy-kershaw-cma-red-carpet-new-music-memoir/)

## Thursday, Nov. 19

- **FAA online meetings,** 1-3 p.m. and 5:30-7:30 p.m., the last two.

## Thursday to Saturday, Nov. 19-21: playoff football

The regional round of the state playoffs is set for Nov. 19-21, according to the LHSAA. Schools set the exact kickoff times, and we'll post them when they're listed.

- **Kaplan** (No. 4, Division III Non-Select) **hosts No. 13 Vidalia.**
- **Delcambre** (No. 25, Division IV Non-Select), fresh off a 19-18 upset, **plays at No. 9 Grand Lake.** [Last week's scores.](/news/playoff-scores-delcambre-upsets-south-plaquemines/)

## Friday to Sunday, Nov. 20-22

- **"Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat," final weekend,** at the Abbey Players theater, 200 S. State St., Abbeville: Friday and Saturday at 7 p.m. and a Sunday matinee at 2 p.m. Tickets are $20. [Showtimes and cast.](/news/abbey-players-joseph-technicolor-dreamcoat-abbeville/)

## Saturday, Nov. 21

- **Last day to register online to vote in Gueydan's Dec. 12 mayor's runoff,** through the state's GeauxVote system. Early voting starts the next Saturday, Nov. 28. [All the dates.](/news/gueydan-mayor-runoff-voter-deadlines-early-voting/)
