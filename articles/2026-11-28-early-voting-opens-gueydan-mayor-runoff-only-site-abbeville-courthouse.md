Gueydan voters can start picking their next mayor today.

Early voting for the Dec. 12 runoff opened Saturday morning. There is one catch: you cannot do it in Gueydan. The Louisiana Secretary of State lists one early voting site for all of Vermilion Parish, and it is in Abbeville.

## Where and when

- **Where:** Vermilion Parish Courthouse, 100 N. State St., Suite 120, Abbeville.
- **When:** 8:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., today through **Saturday, Dec. 5**.
- **Closed:** Sunday, Nov. 29.
- **Phone:** 337-898-4324

If you would sooner vote close to home, wait for election day. Polls in Gueydan are open **Saturday, Dec. 12, from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.**

## Who is on the ballot

The mayor's race is the only thing on the ballot in Vermilion Parish, according to the Secretary of State's candidate list. Only Gueydan voters have something to vote on.

- **Jason Suire**, Republican
- **Efrem "Zim" Livingston**, Democrat

Both are town aldermen.

## Why every vote counts

Nobody won a majority on Nov. 3, so the top two moved on. It was about as close as a three-way race gets:

- Suire: 133 votes
- Livingston: 121 votes
- Jeffrey Lamson: 115 votes

Twelve votes separated the two men now in the runoff. The 115 votes that went to Lamson are now up for grabs.

## Voting by mail

- **Tuesday, Dec. 8, 4:30 p.m.:** deadline to ask for a mail ballot. Military and overseas voters have different rules.
- **Friday, Dec. 11, 4:30 p.m.:** the registrar must have your finished ballot by then.

You can check your registration, polling place and sample ballot at [voterportal.sos.la.gov](https://voterportal.sos.la.gov).

## Also this week in Gueydan

The town holds a public hearing on its budget **Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 5:45 p.m.** at City Hall, with a council meeting at 6 p.m. Both candidates sit on that board. [Read our story on the town's budget.](/news/gueydan-budget-hearing-utility-transfers-fire-station/)

The Vermilion Times will post results on election night.
