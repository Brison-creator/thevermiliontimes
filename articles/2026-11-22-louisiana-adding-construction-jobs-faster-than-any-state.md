Louisiana had 153,400 construction jobs in October. A year earlier it had 135,800.

That is 17,600 more people working construction in this state in one year, a 13% jump, according to federal jobs numbers. The Vermilion Times compared the same numbers for every state. No other state grew nearly that fast.

## How Louisiana compares

- **Louisiana: up 13%.**
- **Next closest, Iowa: up 8.4%.**
- **The whole country: up 1.5%.** The nation added about 124,000 construction jobs in the year. About one of every seven was in Louisiana.

Only two states added more construction jobs by count, and both are far bigger: Texas, with 26,900, and Ohio, with 18,000.

## Construction is carrying the state

Louisiana as a whole added about 40,000 jobs of all kinds in the year. More than four of every 10 were in construction.

Construction work here is close to its highest level on record. The peak, in numbers that go back to 1990, was 154,600 jobs in June 2018. September's count of 153,800 was the second highest ever. October slipped by 400.

## Closer to home

The federal numbers do not break out Vermilion Parish by itself. They count it as part of the Lafayette metro area.

- **Construction jobs in the Lafayette area:** about 12,400 in October, up from 11,000 a year earlier. That is roughly 1,400 more, also a 13% gain.
- **All jobs in the Lafayette area:** 190,700, up 2,400 from a year earlier.

## What the numbers don't say

They do not say which projects are doing the hiring, what the jobs pay, or how many of the workers live here. The October figures are preliminary and can be revised.
