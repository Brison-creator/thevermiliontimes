SpaceX has started hiring security for its Vermilion Parish site.

Two security jobs based in **Pecan Island** appeared on the company's careers board this week. They are the first security jobs the Times has seen listed for the Louisiana project. They bring the number of SpaceX jobs listed at Pecan Island to **12**.

## The two jobs

**Sr. Physical Security Hardware Specialist** (posted Nov. 17). This is the person who puts in and keeps up the security equipment: door access systems, security cameras and the video system behind them. SpaceX asks for a high school diploma or equivalent and at least five years working on security systems such as cameras, access control or alarms. The listing also calls for a valid driver's license, the ability to lift 35 pounds and a background check that allows access to facilities on Defense Department installations.

**Sr. Physical Security Specialist** (posted Nov. 18). This one is closer to a bodyguard and security team lead. The listing describes protecting senior company leaders, watching for surveillance, sizing up risks and handling security for large company events. SpaceX asks for a high school diploma or equivalent, at least eight years in the military, law enforcement, overseas security contracting or advanced corporate security, and two years of executive protection or emergency response team work. Travel on short notice, up to 30% of the time, is part of the job.

## What else is listed at Pecan Island

The other 10 openings are for engineers and managers getting the site ready: construction project management and construction safety, land development, materials management, environmental engineering and permitting, environmental health and safety, and a communications lead to work with the surrounding communities.

The oldest of those listings dates to mid-September. Most were posted in October and November.

## How to apply

Every listing is on SpaceX's careers page, spacex.com/careers. Search for "Pecan Island." SpaceX says applicants for these jobs must be U.S. citizens, permanent residents or otherwise eligible under federal export rules.
