If the App Store wouldn't load on your iPhone this morning, or Apple Music stopped playing, it isn't your phone.

**Update, 4:50 p.m. Central:** Most of Apple's services are back. Its status pages now list **iCloud.com** as fixed at 3:15 p.m., **Apple School Manager, Schoolwork and Apple Business** at 4 p.m., and **Xcode Cloud** at 4:30 p.m.; News Publisher and the Volume Purchase Program are also marked resolved. Still listed as down: **iCloud sign-in**, and for developers **App Store Connect** with its analytics and sales reports, the Developer ID Notary Service and Apple's music tools for artists.

**Update, 3:55 p.m. Central:** Little has changed in the past half hour. Apple's status pages still list problems with **iCloud sign-in and iCloud.com**, Apple School Manager and Apple Business, and on the developer side with **App Store Connect** and its analytics and sales reports, Xcode Cloud, the Developer ID Notary Service, Apple's music tools for artists and News Publisher. The Volume Purchase Program, which came back at 2 p.m., has had on-and-off trouble again since 3:11 p.m. The App Store and Apple Music are still listed as working.

**Update, 3:25 p.m. Central: Apple says an internal update caused it.** In a statement shared with Bloomberg's Mark Gurman and reported by MacRumors at 1:14 p.m. Central, Apple said "many of our systems are back up and running after an internal update caused some outages this morning," that it is "working to get the remaining systems online as soon as possible," and that it was not a cyberattack.

**Update, 3:10 p.m. Central:** Apple now lists **Schoolwork** as fixed. Xcode Cloud's first outage was marked resolved at 3 p.m. Central, but Apple opened a new Xcode Cloud outage at the same minute, and it added the **Developer ID Notary Service**, which Mac developers use to clear their apps for release, as having problems since 10:25 a.m. Still down: iCloud sign-in and iCloud.com, Apple School Manager, Apple Business, and App Store Connect with its analytics and sales reports.

**Update, 2:57 p.m. Central:** The App Store is back. Apple's status page now lists the App Store outage as resolved at **2 p.m. Central**, after about three hours and 15 minutes; the Volume Purchase Program came back at the same time, and AppleCare purchases on devices were restored at 2:39 p.m. Still listed as down: **iCloud sign-in and iCloud.com**, Apple School Manager, Schoolwork and Apple Business, and on the developer side **App Store Connect** (including its analytics and sales reports), Xcode Cloud, Apple's music tools for artists and News Publisher.

**Update, 1:42 p.m. Central:** The outage is spreading. Apple's status page now lists the **App Store** as down again, starting at **1:27 p.m. Central**, along with the Volume Purchase Program that businesses and schools use to buy apps in bulk. **iCloud.com** web apps and iCloud sign-in are still having problems; Apple lists iCloud Mail as fixed. On the developer side, App Store Connect's analytics and sales reports went down at 1:09 p.m., and so did Apple Music's tools for artists. Apple School Manager, Schoolwork and Apple Business remain down.

**Update, 12:55 p.m. Central:** Apple's status page no longer lists a problem with the App Store or Apple Music. But new outages appeared at **12:40 p.m. Central** for **Apple School Manager** and **Schoolwork**, the tools many schools use to manage student iPads and assignments, and for **Apple Business**. iCloud sign-in, AppleCare purchases, App Store Connect and Xcode Cloud are still listed as having problems.

Apple's own system status page lists an **ongoing outage of the App Store and Apple Music** starting at **10:45 a.m. Central Tuesday**, along with an issue with **iCloud account sign-in** that began at the same time. Apple says those services "may be slow or unavailable." Buying AppleCare on a device has been down since 10:15 a.m., according to the page.

## Developers are locked out, too

On Apple's separate status page for developers, **App Store Connect**, the site app makers use to publish apps, push updates and read their sales, has been out since **10:50 a.m. Central**. Apple marks it "All users are affected." **Xcode Cloud**, Apple's service for building and testing apps, went down at 11 a.m., also for all users, and the News Publisher tool is slowed.

Apple's message on the developer services: "Users are experiencing a problem with this service. We are working to resolve this issue."

Earlier in the morning, in-app purchases also had trouble for some users from 4:28 to 5:46 a.m. Central. Apple lists that problem as resolved.

## What it means for you

- Downloading or updating apps from the App Store may fail until Apple fixes it.
- If iCloud won't let you sign in, it's a known problem: Apple lists it on its status page.
- App developers can't publish updates or check their stores until App Store Connect comes back.

Apple has blamed an internal update and ruled out a cyberattack, but it hasn't said when every service will be back. The Times will update this story when Apple's status page changes.
